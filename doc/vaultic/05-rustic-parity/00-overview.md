Vaultic aims to reach feature parity with [rustic](https://github.com/rustic-rs/rustic)
while keeping its own strengths. The purpose, scope, non-goals, and guiding
principles for this effort are defined once, alongside the vaulticdb metadata
service's own purpose, in
[Vision, Scope, and Non-Negotiable Guarantees](../01-strategy/01-vision-and-principles.md#part-b--rustic-feature-parity).
