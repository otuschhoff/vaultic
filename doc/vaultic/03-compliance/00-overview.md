# Compliance Overview

[← Back to index](../README.md)

Vaultic and vaulticdb are assessed against six regulatory, cybersecurity, and
governance frameworks, plus the shared security/verification architecture.
These assessments are supported by [Phase 16 and Phase 17](../04-roadmap/05-analytics-compliance-scale-phases-16-20.md)
of the roadmap. They assess vaultic's technical enablement and evidence; they
do not certify a deploying organization or replace its governance and
operational controls.

## Documents in this section

1. [GDPR Compliance Assessment](01-gdpr.md) — EU GDPR 2016/679 article-by-article
   analysis and operational SOPs.
2. [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Compliance Assessment](02-iso27001.md) — ISMS control mapping
   and maturity ratings.
3. [NIS2 Compliance Assessment](03-nis2.md) — EU Directive (EU) 2022/2555 Article 21
   risk-management measure mapping and Article 23 incident-reporting readiness.
4. [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 Assessment](05-nist-csf-2.0.md) — Govern,
   Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover outcome mapping.
5. [CIS Critical Security Controls v8.1 Assessment](06-cis-controls-v8.md) — all
   18 Controls, Implementation Group guidance, and responsibility boundaries.
6. [COBIT 2019 Governance and Management Assessment](07-cobit-2019.md) — EDM,
   APO, BAI, DSS, and MEA objective enablement.
7. [Security & Verification Architecture](08-security-and-verification-architecture.md) —
   Azure Key Vault Option A, multi-target syslog, per-chunk GDPR erasure analysis,
   and sampled storage verification.
