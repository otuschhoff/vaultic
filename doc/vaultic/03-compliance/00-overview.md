# Compliance Overview

[← Back to index](../README.md)

Vaultic and vaulticdb are assessed against two regulatory/standards frameworks,
plus the security/verification architecture that both rely on. These assessments
are implemented by [Phase 16 and Phase 17](../04-roadmap/05-analytics-compliance-scale-phases-16-19.md)
of the roadmap.

## Documents in this section

1. [GDPR Compliance Assessment](01-gdpr.md) — EU GDPR 2016/679 article-by-article
   analysis and operational SOPs.
2. [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Compliance Assessment](02-iso27001.md) — ISMS control mapping
   and maturity ratings.
3. [Security & Verification Architecture](03-security-and-verification-architecture.md) —
   Azure Key Vault Option A, multi-target syslog, per-chunk GDPR erasure analysis,
   and sampled storage verification.
